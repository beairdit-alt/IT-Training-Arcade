Cybersecurity Technician Challenge

This simulator is built around the current Information Technology Specialist Cybersecurity objectives. It covers security principles and frameworks, network security, endpoint protection, vulnerability and risk management, and incident handling. Questions are original and the skills labs are safe defensive simulations.

1. Security Principles

Hardening, defense-in-depth, CIA, ethics, zero trust, frameworks, threats, social engineering, AAA, MFA, encryption, PKI.

2. Securing the Network

Protocol risks, segmentation, NAT, DMZ/VLAN, firewalls, ACLs, IDS/IPS, VPN, NAC, wireless security and security logs.

3. Protecting Endpoints

OS hardening, permissions, Defender, endpoint tools, packet capture, patching, compliance, system logs and malware removal.

4. Vulnerability & Risk Management

Threat intelligence, CVEs, severity, risk, mitigations, assessments, reconnaissance, scanning and penetration-test process.

5. Incident Handling

SIEM/SOAR, true/false positives, digital forensics, chain of custody, MITRE ATT&CK, incident response, compliance, DR/BCP and recovery.
