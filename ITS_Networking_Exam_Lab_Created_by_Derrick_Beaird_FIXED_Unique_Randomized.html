Networking Technician Challenge

This simulator follows the current Information Technology Specialist Networking objective structure. It covers foundational network concepts, LAN/WAN and wireless infrastructure, switches/routers/cabling, OSI/TCP-IP/IPv4/IPv6/ports/DNS/DHCP/NAT, and Windows/Linux troubleshooting tools.

1. Networking Fundamentals

Internet/intranet/extranet, client-server, peer-to-peer, unicast/multicast/broadcast, IoT, virtualization, VPN and Remote Desktop.

2. Network Infrastructures

LANs, DMZs, VLANs, WANs, DSL/cable/satellite/cellular, wireless standards/security/interference, topologies.

3. Network Hardware

Switches, access/trunk ports, Layer 2/3, MAC tables, STP, routers, routes, QoS, cabling, fiber, EMI and crosstalk.

4. Protocols & Services

OSI/TCP-IP models, IPv4/IPv6, ports, DNS records, name resolution, DHCP, NAT/PAT and firewalls.

5. Troubleshooting

Process, professional conduct, cable tools, TDR/OTDR, Windows and Linux diagnostic commands.
